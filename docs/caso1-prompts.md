# Caso 1 — prompts de la cinemática del prólogo (4 imágenes)

Formato: 4:3 (1024×768). Al terminar, mándalas en orden (1 a 4) y se ponen en `assets/cine/` como `c1_1.png` … `c1_4.png`.
Regla del caso: **no mostrar la cara de nadie** (el jugador no debe saber quién fue), pero sí dejar las pistas justas:
el cuadro grande, el corte de luz, algo amarillo DENTRO de la galería con la luz verde de emergencia, y el vidrio que cae HACIA AFUERA.

## Estilo (va al inicio de las 4)
Anime visual novel cutscene illustration, high-detail anime style with crisp dark outlines and soft painterly shading, like a polished 2000s Japanese courtroom adventure game. Night scene in Valparaíso, Chile, deep blue and violet palette with warm accents. 4:3 aspect ratio (1024x768), cinematic composition, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no visible faces.

## 1 — «Cerro Alegre, Valparaíso. 3 de noviembre de 2030.»
Wide establishing shot at night of Cerro Alegre in Valparaíso: steep streets, colorful wooden houses with corrugated metal facades climbing the hill, the harbor lights and the sea below. In the foreground, a small two-story art gallery with a sign reading only a simple painted mermaid symbol (no letters), a street lamp lit in front, a half-painted colorful mural on its outside wall with a small wooden scaffold. Through the gallery's small street window you can glimpse a large framed oil painting of a mermaid inside. Calm, quiet, a little melancholic.

## 2 — «23:10. Un apagón deja la galería a oscuras.»
The same street and gallery seconds after a blackout: every window, the street lamp and the whole hillside go dark at once, only moonlight remains. Blue and black tones, the silhouettes of the houses against a starry sky, a cat on a wall looking up startled. Across the street, an elderly woman's balcony with potted plants, dark and empty for now. Ominous silence.

## 3 — «Un ruido en el interior. Pasos que nadie consigue identificar.»
Interior of the dark gallery: only a small green emergency exit light glows on the wall near the street window. In the green glow, something large wrapped in a bright yellow tarp, almost two meters tall, is being dragged toward a side corridor door; the person carrying it is completely hidden behind the tarp and the darkness, no face, no recognizable features, only a vague shadow and a pair of shoes. On the wall, an empty space where a large painting used to hang, with the pale rectangle it left behind. Tense, mysterious.

## 4 — «Un vidrio se rompe. Al día siguiente, "La Sirena del Puerto" ha desaparecido.»
Dramatic close-up from the street side of the gallery's small window (about 60 by 80 centimeters) shattering at night: the glass fragments burst OUTWARD toward the viewer and rain down onto the sidewalk, as if hit from inside. Behind the broken window the room is pitch black, nobody visible. Moonlight sparkles on the flying shards, motion lines, impact flash, strong sense of a sudden sound breaking the silence.
